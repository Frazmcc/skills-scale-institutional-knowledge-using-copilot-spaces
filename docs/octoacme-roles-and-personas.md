# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance (QA) Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads own quality strategy and release confidence. They ensure planned work is testable, validated against acceptance criteria, and ready for production use.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy across feature, integration, and regression coverage
- Partner with developers to build maintainable automated and manual test plans
- Validate readiness against acceptance criteria and quality gates
- Track defects, triage severity, and coordinate retest priorities
- Provide release quality sign-off input to project and product leadership

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching customers
- Shorten feedback loops on quality risks
- Improve confidence in release readiness decisions

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality checklists in shared project docs
- Defect triage updates with developers and project managers
- Release readiness reviews with PMs, Product Managers, and stakeholders

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with **Developers** to clarify edge cases, automate tests, and resolve defects quickly.
- Partner with **Product Managers** to verify acceptance criteria are testable and outcome-aligned.
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** on quality risk visibility, timelines, and go/no-go checkpoints.

---

## Engineering Managers / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers and Technical Leads provide technical direction, staffing alignment, and architectural oversight to support predictable delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design decisions and architecture trade-offs
- Align engineering capacity with roadmap and delivery commitments
- Remove technical blockers and escalate systemic engineering risks
- Coach developers on standards, maintainability, and operational excellence
- Ensure non-functional requirements (security, reliability, performance) are addressed

### Goals
- Keep delivery technically sound and sustainable
- Reduce rework caused by late architecture or dependency issues
- Improve team throughput while maintaining quality

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture notes
- Dependency and staffing discussions with Project Managers and Product Managers
- Risk and escalation updates to stakeholders when delivery is threatened

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with **Developers** on implementation patterns, reviews, and technical mentorship.
- Partner with **Project Managers** on dependency planning, sequencing, and risk escalation.
- Align with **Product Managers** on scope trade-offs that protect delivery outcomes.

---

## Business Stakeholders / Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Business Stakeholders and Executive Sponsors define strategic direction and approve key delivery decisions that affect business outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Set success criteria, budget constraints, and business priorities
- Provide timely decisions on scope, timeline, and risk trade-offs
- Approve major milestones and release readiness from a business perspective
- Sponsor cross-functional support when escalations require organizational action
- Review outcomes and confirm value realization after delivery

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Keep delivery aligned with strategic priorities
- Reduce decision latency during critical project moments

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and steering updates
- Decision logs for scope, risk, and investment choices
- Outcome and KPI reviews with Product and Project Managers

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with **Product Managers** to align roadmap outcomes with business priorities.
- Rely on **Project Managers** for schedule, risk, and dependency transparency.
- Engage with **Developers** and **QA Leads** during milestone demos and release decisions when needed.
